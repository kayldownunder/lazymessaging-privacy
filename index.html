Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

KaylDownUnder built the LazyMessaging app as a free app. This Service is provided by KaylDownUnder at no cost and is intended for use as is.

What LazyMessaging does with your data

LazyMessaging does not have its own server, does not create user accounts, and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to KaylDownUnder.

The one exception is the optional "Fix spelling & punctuation" feature. If you choose to use it, the message text currently on your screen is sent directly from your device to the AI provider you've selected in Settings — Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (ChatGPT), or Google (Gemini) — using an API key you provide yourself. LazyMessaging never sees or stores that text; it is sent straight from your device to that provider and back. Review the relevant provider's own privacy policy for how they handle it:

  • Anthropic (Claude)
  • OpenAI (ChatGPT)
  • Google (Gemini)

If you don't add an API key or don't use the Polish feature, no message text ever leaves your device through LazyMessaging.

Microphone and voice dictation

LazyMessaging asks for the microphone permission only so you can dictate a message by voice. Recording starts only when you tap the microphone button. Speech-to-text is performed by your device's built-in speech recognition service (on most Android phones, Google's), which may process audio on your device or send it to that service's servers depending on your device and settings — see Google's Privacy Policy. LazyMessaging itself does not record, save, or transmit your audio; it only receives the resulting text and places it in the message box. You can deny or revoke the microphone permission at any time and still type your messages.

Local data storage

LazyMessaging has no accounts and no server-side storage. Your API key(s) and display preferences (text size, font, colour, background) are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. This settings file, including your API keys, is excluded from Android's automatic backup and device-transfer features, so it never leaves your device even when your phone backs up or transfers to a new one — each install starts blank, and you add your own key. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

Sending messages

When you tap Send, LazyMessaging opens your phone's own Messages, WhatsApp, Messenger, Teams, or Email app (or a general share sheet) with your text pre-filled. You choose the recipient and send it yourself — LazyMessaging does not send anything automatically and has no visibility into who you send it to.

Advertising and subscriptions

LazyMessaging does not currently show ads or offer a paid subscription. If we add advertising (via Google AdMob) or a subscription (via Google Play Billing) in the future, this policy will be updated first to name the specific services used and what data they collect, and the update will be dated at the top of this page.

Children's Privacy

This Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
